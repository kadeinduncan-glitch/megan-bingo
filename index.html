Megan's Baby Bingo

Send her off on leave right. Tap a square when you spot it at the send-off. First to get five in a row, across, down or diagonal, calls it out.

How to play

This game is played online, live, during Megan's virtual send-off. Players are the coworkers on the call. The host is whoever is running the send-off.

  1. Each player: open this page on your own phone or laptop. No sign-in needed. You get your own shuffled card. Do not refresh the page, or your card reshuffles and your marks are lost.
  2. Each player: when you see or hear something on your card happen during the send-off, tap that square. It turns yellow. The center square is free and already marked.
  3. Each player: when you have five yellow squares in a row (across, down or diagonal), the row gets an outline and a Bingo banner appears. Type "BINGO" in the group chat and post a screenshot of your card.
  4. Host: check the screenshot. All five squares in the row must be yellow (or the free center), and each one must have really happened on the call.
  5. Host: if the row checks out, announce the winner in the chat and arrange the prize. If it does not, tell the player in the chat, and the game goes on.

Rules

  • Only mark a square after you see or hear it happen yourself.
  • Marked a square by mistake? Tap it again to unmark it.
  • First confirmed Bingo wins. If two players post at nearly the same time, the earlier message in the chat wins.
  • Your card lives only on your own screen. Nobody else can see it, so the screenshot is your proof.
  • Keep it kind. This is a party for Megan.

"Deal me a new card" gives you a fresh shuffle. "Clear marks" keeps your card and removes your marks.
