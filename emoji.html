Megan's Emoji Decoder

Ten emoji puzzles, baby and leave edition. Pick the phrase that fits. Play on your own, then post your score in the group chat.

How to play
  • Everyone plays on their own screen. No sign-in needed.
  • Tap an answer. It locks in and shows if you were right.
  • Tap Next. After 10 puzzles you get your score.
  • Post your score in the group chat. Highest score wins. Ties go to the earlier post.
  • Kadein is the host and settles any dispute.
